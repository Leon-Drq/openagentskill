# Governance

OpenAgentSkill uses a maintainer-led, evidence-driven governance model.

## Roles

### Maintainers

Maintainers set product direction, review and merge changes, manage releases, respond to security reports, and protect the integrity of registry, audit, ranking, and outcome data.

### Contributors

Contributors improve code, documentation, tests, metadata, research, and community support through issues and pull requests. Consistent, high-quality contributors may be invited to take on triage or review responsibilities.

### Skill authors and users

Skill authors can submit and claim listings, correct metadata, and provide evidence. Users and agents can report outcomes and risks. Popularity, authorship, sponsorship, or promotional relationships do not guarantee ranking or approval.

## Decision making

- Routine changes are decided through pull-request review and automated checks.
- Product and ranking decisions should include the user or agent workflow, alternatives considered, expected impact, and measurable evidence where practical.
- Security, privacy, legal, and data-integrity concerns can override growth or compatibility goals.
- Material changes to Trust Score, ranking methodology, telemetry, or install policy must be documented in the pull request and public documentation.

When consensus is not possible, the lead maintainer makes the final decision and records the reasoning in the relevant issue, pull request, release note, or roadmap.

## Conflicts of interest

Maintainers and contributors must disclose material relationships with a Skill, repository, vendor, or competitor when participating in ranking, audit, or enforcement decisions. OpenAgentSkill does not sell hidden ranking placement.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes use the normal pull-request process and should remain open long enough for meaningful community review unless an urgent security or legal issue requires faster action.
